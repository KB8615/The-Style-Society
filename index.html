Dressed to express. Built to be seen.

The Style Society is Chitkara University's home for people who treat getting dressed as an art form. We are dedicated to fashion, expression and creativity, to the high-life with its glamour, its gloss and its grand entrances, and to the freedom and fun of becoming exactly who you want to be.

Here, a silhouette is a statement, a thrifted jacket is a plot twist, and nobody has to ask permission to shine.

Five things we live for

Every shoot, show and styling session we run comes back to these.

Fashion

Clothes are our first language. We study the silhouette, chase the trend and break it the moment it gets comfortable.

Expression

What you wear says something before you do. We help you say it louder, truer and entirely on your own terms.

Creativity

Mood boards, styling shoots, upcycled looks and wild ideas. A studio of imaginative minds turning sparks into statements.

The High-Life

Glamour, gloss and the grand entrance. We believe in living beautifully, and bringing a little luxury to campus.

Freedom and Fun

There's no dress code for your personality. Play, experiment, laugh, and be gloriously, unapologetically yourself.
